# Third-party assets

## Armored knight

- Asset: **Knight**, by **piacenti**.
- Original: https://opengameart.org/content/knight-2
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported** (CC BY 3.0).
- License terms: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Downloaded source: `armored knight.zip`, containing `armor.blend` and packed 2048 × 2048 albedo, metallic, roughness and normal textures.
- Used in: the player and auxiliary guards; sword and shield equipment.
- Changes for Castle Last Watch: restored PBR node material, normalized scale, separated shield, added a 15-bone deformation rig and weights, generated two distance LOD meshes, repositioned weapon attachment origins, exported FBX, and added Unity material and procedural movement/combat pose integration.

The source geometry and texture artwork are by piacenti; these adaptations do not imply endorsement by the original author. Preserve this attribution and license link when distributing this demo or its derived assets. The packaged game includes a copy of this notice.

## Environment PBR textures (CC0)
- Poly Haven: Medieval Blocks 03 by Rob Tuytel, https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_blocks_03. Color, normal and roughness maps at 2K.
- ambientCG: Wood066 and Ground037, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood066 and https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground037. Color, normal and roughness at 1K.
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0; https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ and https://polyhaven.com/license.
Enemy soldiers reuse the previously credited Knight by piacenti with local cloth and equipment modifications.

## Underground quarry scan (CC0)
- Poly Haven Rock Face 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_01.
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license.
- Downloaded the glTF mesh and 2K diffuse, normal and packed AO/roughness/metal maps.
- Adapted into quarry wall, ceiling, vein and rubble modules; added original timber supports, ironwork, tools, track and mining cart in Blender.
- Poly Haven Gravel Floor and Rock Wall 10: https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor and https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_wall_10, CC0. 2K albedo, normal and roughness textures for continuous quarry surfaces.

## Detailed enemy adaptation
The enemy v02 retains the credited Knight's anatomical armour and UV texture atlas. The replacement bascinet with eye slit, individual linked mail aventail, folded crimson tabard, rivets and distance LODs are local Blender adaptations. The player and allied guards retain the original knight silhouette.
